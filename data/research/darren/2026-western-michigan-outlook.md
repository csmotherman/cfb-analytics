# Western Michigan 2026 — Darren Scouting Outlook

**Purpose:** numbers-first opponent outlook for Darren. This file separates (1) repository/model output, (2) roster-continuity calculations, and (3) externally researched 2026 staff/scheme/personnel context so the numbers are easy to quote without mixing methodologies.

## 0. One-minute overview

- **2025 finish:** Western Michigan finished **10-4**, won the **MAC Championship**, and beat Kennesaw State in the Myrtle Beach Bowl. The repository's FBS-only tendency/model sample is **9-4 over 13 games** because the Rhode Island FCS game is not part of the published FBS sample.
- **2025 identity:** the offense was extremely run-oriented — **65.1% rush decision rate**, **42.85 rush attempts/game**, **23.0 dropbacks/game**. The defense carried the team: opponent-adjusted composite **#34 nationally** versus **#100 offense**.
- **2026 continuity:** ESPN returning production is **51% overall, 60% offense, 42% defense**. CBS returning snaps are **43% overall, 52% offense, 33% defense**. Our official-roster headcount audit finds **51 of 104 players (49.0%)** return from the 2025 roster.
- **Where continuity lives:** CBS has WMU returning **89% of QB snaps, 70% RB, 61% WR, 2% TE, 52% OL**. On defense: **20% DL, 12% LB, 55% DB**. That is a very clear roster story: the offensive backfield/WR core is stable; TE and the defensive front seven are being rebuilt.
- **Staff:** **Lance Taylor** remains HC. **Walt Bell** remains OC. **Greer Martini** was promoted from 2025 linebackers coach to DC after Chris O'Leary left for the Los Angeles Chargers; **Duane Vaughn** was added as co-DC/OLBs.
- **Scheme headline:** offense = **run-heavy, QB-run-centered multiple attack** built around Broc Lowry and the ground game. Defense = **continuity-based, attack/havoc-oriented scheme** under a new playcaller rather than a wholesale system change.

# 1. Staff and 2026 structural changes

## Head coach — Lance Taylor

- **2026 is Year 4 at WMU.**
- Won **2025 MAC Coach of the Year**.
- WMU won the 2025 MAC title in Taylor's third season.
- Contract extended through the **2030 season** after the championship.

**Darren shorthand:** `Lance Taylor — Year 4; reigning MAC Coach of the Year; program continuity at HC.`

## Offensive coordinator — Walt Bell

- **Walt Bell — Offensive Coordinator / QB coach.**
- Hired in January 2024, so 2026 is his **third season running the WMU offense**.
- Bell and QB **Broc Lowry** were together at Indiana before both landed at WMU.
- WMU's own 2025 feature on Lowry quotes Bell saying the staff would make "small evolutions" to the system to fit Lowry and the offensive line rather than wholesale weekly changes.
- Lowry finished 2025 as MAC Offensive Player of the Year: **1,803 passing yards, 9 pass TD; 963 rushing yards, 14 rush TD**.

**Darren shorthand:** `Walt Bell returns. The offense is not resetting; the playcaller and the quarterback that drove the 2025 run game are both back.`

## Defensive coordinator — Greer Martini

- **Greer Martini — Defensive Coordinator / LBs.**
- Joined WMU as linebackers coach in January 2025.
- Promoted to DC on **Feb. 11, 2026**.
- Replaces **Chris O'Leary**, who became the Los Angeles Chargers defensive coordinator on Jan. 28, 2026.
- O'Leary's 2025 WMU defense allowed **17.4 points/game (#9 FBS)** and **305.5 yards/game (#19 FBS)** per the Chargers' official announcement.
- Martini is an internal promotion. Players interviewed by Mitten Football described the transition as **"different person, same scheme"**; Martini said spring was about seeing which scheme pieces fit the new personnel while maintaining the standard established in 2025.

## Co-defensive coordinator — Duane Vaughn

- Added March 27, 2026 as **OLBs / Co-Defensive Coordinator**.
- Came from Furman after **13 seasons**, including **five as defensive coordinator**.

**Darren shorthand:** `New defensive playcaller, but not a clean-sheet install. Martini coached in the 2025 system; Vaughn adds experienced DC support.`

# 5. Offensive scheme and play-calling identity

## Best evidence-backed label

**RUN-HEAVY, QB-RUN-CENTERED MULTIPLE OFFENSE**

Avoid overcomplicating this for the overview. The 2025 numbers tell the story more clearly than jargon.

### 2025 play-calling / decision tendency

Repository raw weighted season totals:

- **Rush decision rate: 65.1%**
- **Dropback rate: 34.9%**
- **42.85 rush attempts/game**
- **23.00 dropbacks/game**
- **62.85 offensive plays/game**
- **10.69 possessions/game**

Michigan's 2025 comparison:

- Michigan rush decision rate: **57.1%**
- WMU was therefore **8.0 percentage points more rush-oriented** by this decision-rate definition.

### Why the QB matters to the scheme

Lowry did not merely scramble occasionally. He rushed for **963 yards and 14 TD**, the most rushing yards by a WMU quarterback in program history according to Bell's official bio.

The offense increasingly centered on the things Lowry and the OL did best after he became the full-time starter. WMU's own feature quoted Bell saying the system would keep making small evolutions to fit Lowry and the line. In the Toledo comeback, WMU explicitly leaned on its core run-game plays near the goal line, and contemporary game analysis identified a Lowry zone-read keeper on the winning sequence.

### Do not oversell the passing game

2025 opponent-adjusted offense:

- **Success rate: 40.3% — #95/136**
- **Rush success: 42.7% — #65**
- **Pass success: 38.3% — #118**
- **Explosive-play rate: 10.9% — #80**
- **Yards/play: 4.91 — #119**

Research-only supporting metrics:

- **Rush explosive rate: 12.8% — #84**
- **Pass explosive rate: 8.5% — #112**
- **Rush YPA: 4.78 — #78**
- **Net pass yards/dropback: 5.22 — #126**
- **Passing-down success: 26.6% — #126**

**Offensive summary:** WMU was not a nationally efficient offense in 2025. The value came from the run-heavy identity, Lowry's legs, situational control and the ability to win with defense rather than consistently creating efficient dropback offense.

**SAME FOUNDATION, NEW PLAYCALLER; ATTACK/HAVOC EMPHASIS**

**Defensive summary:** the scheme/organizational knowledge survives better than the front-seven production. # 8. What opponent adjustment changes

Raw numbers make WMU's defense look elite and the offense modest. Opponent adjustment preserves the broad conclusion but adds nuance.

## Offense

- Raw success **42.5%** becomes adjusted **40.3% (#95)**.
- Raw rush success **45.1%** becomes adjusted **42.7% (#65)**.
- Raw pass success **37.5%** becomes adjusted **38.3% (#118)**.
- Raw YPP **5.18** becomes adjusted **4.91 (#119)**.

**Interpretation:** schedule context does not rescue the 2025 offense. It remained a bottom-quarter-ish efficiency unit overall, with the run game clearly stronger than the pass game.

## Defense

- Raw success allowed **38.1%** becomes adjusted **39.8% (#35)**.
- Raw rush success allowed **40.0%** becomes adjusted **41.1% (#43)**.
- Raw pass success allowed **36.1%** becomes adjusted **38.4% (#23)**.
- Raw YPP allowed **5.30** becomes adjusted **5.57 (#52)**.

**Interpretation:** the defense was legitimately good after opponent adjustment, especially at suppressing pass success. It was not equally dominant in every dimension; rush explosiveness and rush YPA were much less impressive in the research-only adjusted layer.

# 9. Game-by-game opponent-adjusted trajectory

Game POE is strict leave-one-game-out. Positive = WMU performed better than expected after accounting for opponent and venue.

## The first three FBS games were genuinely bad offensive performances

Offensive success-rate POE:

- Michigan State: **-18.3 percentage points**
- North Texas: **-8.4 pp**
- Illinois: **-15.5 pp**

YPP POE:

- Michigan State: **-2.44 yards/play**
- North Texas: **-1.60**
- Illinois: **-0.72**

This is important because the season average includes an offense that looked materially different before Lowry took over as the clear starter.

## Toledo was the pivot

Vs Toledo — a team our validated-five opponent composite ranks **#16 overall**:

- Offensive success POE: **+3.5 pp**
- Rush success POE: **+1.9 pp**
- Pass success POE: **+4.4 pp**
- YPP POE: **+0.21**

Defense vs Toledo:

- Success POE: **+9.1 pp**
- Pass success POE: **+11.6 pp**
- YPP POE: **+2.11**

That 14-13 win is the cleanest statistical turning point in the season.

## The run game produced the biggest late-season spikes

- At Miami (OH), regular season: **+14.9 pp rush-success POE**, but **-10.5 pp pass-success POE**.
- At Northern Illinois: **+24.2 pp rush-success POE**, while pass-success POE was **-15.5 pp**.

This reinforces the identity: WMU could dramatically outperform expectation on the ground without becoming a consistent dropback passing offense.

## MAC Championship vs Miami (OH)

Offense:

- Success POE: **-10.3 pp**
- Rush success POE: **-17.5 pp**
- Pass success POE: **+11.2 pp**
- Explosive POE: **+2.9 pp**
- YPP POE: **+1.08**

Defense:

- Success POE: **+9.4 pp**
- Rush success POE: **+13.6 pp**
- Pass success POE: **+7.2 pp**
- Explosive POE: **+4.7 pp**
- YPP POE: **+1.02**

**Takeaway:** the title-game defense beat expectation across all five validated measures, while the offense won differently than its normal run-first profile.

# 11. Michigan matchup angles Darren can use

Again: these are based on **2025 statistical strengths plus 2026 roster continuity**, not a game prediction.

## 1. Michigan should force WMU to win through the air

WMU 2025:

- Adjusted rush success offense: **#65**
- Adjusted pass success offense: **#118**
- Net pass yards/dropback: **#126** (research-only)

The obvious defensive priority is to control Lowry's designed/option run value and Buckley, then make WMU sustain drives through conventional dropback passing.

## 2. WMU's defense is more trustworthy on the back end than in the rebuilt front seven

2026 returning snaps:

- DL **20%**
- LB **12%**
- DB **55%**

2025 adjusted defense:

- Pass success defense **#23**
- Rush success defense **#43**
- Rush YPA defense **#107** (research-only)
- Rush explosive defense **#83** (research-only)

For Michigan, the ground game is the most natural place to test whether the 2026 defense can reproduce 2025 results with so many new front-seven bodies.

## 3. The most important WMU player is Lowry by a huge margin

- Reigning MAC Offensive Player of the Year.
- **963 rushing yards / 14 rush TD** in 2025.
- **84.35 total PPA** in our audit.
- **71.5% of returning signed player PPA**.

If Michigan makes Lowry inefficient as a runner, it attacks the central mechanism of the WMU offense rather than just one skill position.

## 4. Do not assume the 2025 WMU defense simply carries over

The scheme foundation carries over, but the personnel distribution does not:

- ESPN defense returning production: **42% (#103)**
- CBS defensive snaps returning: **33% (#94)**
- DL snaps returning: **20%**
- LB snaps returning: **12%**
- 17 defensive college-experience newcomers in our audit

The 2026 defense should be described as **reloaded/reconstructed inside the same general system**, not simply "the same defense."
