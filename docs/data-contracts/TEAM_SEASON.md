# Team Season Contract

Grain: one FBS team in one season. Key: `(season, team_id)`. Identity fields come from `TEAMS`; `games` and every metric numerator/denominator are sums of canonical team-games. Rates are reconstructed after aggregation. Published metric values receive an unambiguous `soar_` alias and national/conference rank and percentile fields from metric metadata. Null means no eligible sample, not zero.
