# Conferences Contract

Membership grain is one observed conference identity; summary grain is one conference/season. Identity columns are conference and deterministic conference slug. Summary columns include season, conference, team count, team-game count, and metric numerators, denominators, and ratios. Ratio metrics are pooled (`sum numerator / sum denominator`), never averages of team rates or ranks. Independents are represented as a grouping when source conference is absent.
